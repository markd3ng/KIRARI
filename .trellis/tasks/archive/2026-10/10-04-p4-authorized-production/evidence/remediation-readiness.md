This resource scope alone does not prove that the token is restricted to the required operations. The [Extended Permissions reference](https://vercel.com/docs/rbac/access-roles/extended-permissions) describes `Full Production Deployment` as allowing CLI Production deployment, rollback, and promotion of any deployment, and lists it as compatible with the Developer team role. [Access Groups](https://vercel.com/docs/rbac/access-groups) assign project roles, cannot override team roles, and effective combinations retain the most permissive permissions. The official docs reviewed do not prove a credential that grants only the required target-project reads plus staged Production deploy/promote/restore while denying unrelated project or team writes. Before creating or accepting any token, the owner must provide effective-permission evidence for exactly those operations, with a token scoped to this project ID alone.
